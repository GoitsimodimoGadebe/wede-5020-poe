Our Mission

Our mission is to protect and improve the lives
of animals by promoting compassion, education, and advocacy.
We work to rescue, rehabilitate, and provide shelter for animals in
need while striving to create a more humane and respectful society for all creatures.


Our Vision

At Animal Welfare Organization(AWO), we envision a future where
no animal is left behind or forgotten. Our mission is to create a world
where every animal, whether stray, abandoned, or abused, receives the care,
compassion, and respect they deserve. We strive to build strong communities
where animal welfare is a priority, and where individuals, families, and
organizations come together to protect and advocate for those who
cannot speak for themselves.
